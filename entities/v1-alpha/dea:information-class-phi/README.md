# Protected Health Information (PHI)

Health information that identifies an individual, created or received in the provision of care, payment, or healthcare operations. Health-privacy regimes govern its use, disclosure, and breach notification.

## Classification
- **Sensitivity:** restricted
- **Regulatory regime:** PHI
- **Jurisdiction anchors:** us-hipaa

## Handling requirements
- Access restricted to workforce members with treatment, payment, or operations roles.
- The minimum-necessary standard applies to all uses and disclosures.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Disclosure logging and breach-notification readiness within statutory deadlines.

## Retention
Per health-records retention statutes in the operating jurisdictions.
