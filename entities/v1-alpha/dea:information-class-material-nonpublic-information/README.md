# Material Non-Public Information (MNPI)

Information that a reasonable investor would consider important to an investment decision, and that is not publicly available. Securities law governs its possession, use, and dissemination; it is restricted without falling under the PII, PHI, or PCI regimes.

## Classification
- **Sensitivity:** restricted
- **Regulatory regime:** none

## Handling requirements
- Access restricted to persons whose duties require it, under a written insider-listing policy.
- Use for personal trading is prohibited; trading windows and restricted lists apply.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Controlled and logged dissemination only; selective disclosure to outsiders requires Regulation-FD-compliant public disclosure.

## Retention
Per the securities-compliance retention schedule; insider lists retained for the statutory period.
