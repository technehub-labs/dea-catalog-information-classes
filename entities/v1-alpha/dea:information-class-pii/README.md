# Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

Information that identifies a natural person, or is capable of doing so when combined with other data. Privacy statutes govern its processing, subject rights, and breach notification.

## Classification
- **Sensitivity:** restricted
- **Regulatory regime:** PII
- **Jurisdiction anchors:** eu-gdpr, uk-gdpr, us-ccpa-cpra

## Handling requirements
- A lawful basis is required for every processing operation.
- Data minimization and purpose limitation apply to all uses.
- Data-subject rights (access, erasure, portability) must be supportable.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Breach-notification readiness within statutory deadlines.

## Retention
Per the applicable privacy statute's limitation periods and the enterprise privacy-retention schedule.
