# Trade Secret

Information deriving economic value from not being generally known and subject to reasonable secrecy efforts. Protected by trade-secret statute rather than a named regulatory regime; disclosure destroys the class's value.

## Classification
- **Sensitivity:** restricted
- **Regulatory regime:** none
- **Jurisdiction anchors:** us-dtsa, eu-trade-secrets-directive

## Handling requirements
- Documented reasonable secrecy efforts (access controls, NDAs, marking) must be maintained; their absence forfeits protection.
- Access restricted to personnel with a demonstrated business need and a contractual confidentiality obligation.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Disclosure events are logged; third-party disclosure requires a written confidentiality agreement.

## Retention
Indefinite while secrecy is maintained and value persists; no statutory retention floor.
